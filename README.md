# Trayi Lumina

We are Trayi Jewellery in Mangalore and I want to design a new website with online e-commerce store. Trayi is an exclusive store selling only LimeLight (https://limelightdiamonds.com/) We need a design like https://www.tanishq.co.in/ - Classy and elegant. While we can use all the collection, pictures and products from Limelight website. Provide more education on Lab grown diamonds and why people choose this and benefits. Emphasise of quality and how it differs from artificial other diamonds - cheap and shiny but fades away. So the customer is aware and be informed on the quality and elegance of lab grown diamonds.   

We need the a login (with social sign-in), cart, purchase feature.  

Use video and content from our Instagram - https://www.instagram.com/trayijewellers/?hl=en
